## Decision Log

### D1 — The thesis (rung 1; owner-ratified 2026-08-08)

The owner's revised position, superseding the earlier
"falsification of Naur" framing (the 2026-07-29 dialogue's "do what
Naur said was impossible" is historical motivation, not the current
account): Naur's strongest claim stands, perhaps tautologically —
theory lives in minds. What automated intelligence adds is not a new
home for theory but a new instrument. Ratified thesis text, verbatim:

> Naur was right — theory lives in minds and, now, in ephemeral
> agent contexts. What automated intelligence adds is not a new home
> for theory but a new instrument: repeatable evaluation that
> articulated portions of a theory are exposed through particular
> code — existence checked deterministically, fidelity judged
> semantically under evidence classes, and unevaluable articulation
> reported rather than guessed. Backstitch measures the transfer
> surface; possession stays where Naur put it.

Consequences accepted with the ratification:

- **Substrate-extension clause accepted**: agent context windows join
  human minds as theory substrates, distinctively *ephemeral* ones;
  the corpus is how theory is re-instantiated into each new substrate,
  so a drifted corpus poisons every rebirth ("false theory is worse
  than absent" survives the revision intact). FLAGGED as a hub
  candidate — this clause is discipline-scope (it extends the hub's
  possession account) and ascends only by owner direction at a hub
  touch, per the hub's ascension boundary.
- **Division of labor with the discipline**: the agent-theory hub
  wagers that theory can be iteratively developed and maintained
  across human and ephemeral-agent substrates; Backstitch makes one
  part of that wager measurable — whether the articulated portions
  still correspond to the code. Neither claim carries the other's
  burden of proof. FLAGGED for a reciprocal line on the hub side at a
  future owner-gated touch.
- **Unifying consequence**: "some portion" makes intent coverage the
  thesis's native measure, not a bolt-on — which portions are
  exposed, which exposures are evaluable (`ambiguous` reports the
  unevaluable ones as a first-class articulation-quality signal),
  which code is exposure of nothing, and how exposure is distributed
  (reach / scatter / concentration as the geometry of the exposure
  relation).
- **Boundary preserved**: evaluation is not possession. Passing every
  gate proves the transfer surface is sound, never that anyone
  understands anything — the same epistemics the operating model
  already holds at process scale, enforced product-side by [SC-16]'s
  evidence-class rule.
